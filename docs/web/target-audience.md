# Target Audience (context.help)

This is the audience definition for the **context.help** homepage and marketing pages.

## Primary audience: Context publishers

Working label: **context specialists**.

They publish curated, structured knowledge that other people and teams can subscribe to.

Examples:

- UX/research practitioners packaging patterns and playbooks
- Security engineers shipping threat intel + mitigation packs
- Domain experts curating "what matters" in fast-moving ecosystems
- Internal enablement teams maintaining "how we do things" registries

Jobs-to-be-done:

- Package expertise into a product that stays current
- Distribute without building infrastructure
- Monetize with subscriptions and (later) credits
- Protect the business model without DRM (traceability, quotas, receipts)
- Provide value beyond PDFs and newsletters (queryable, structured, agent-usable)

Top objections:

- "People will just copy it"
- "This is too technical to publish"
- "How do I get distribution?"
- "Will this work for teams (procurement, SSO, invoicing)?"

## Secondary audience: Small teams (buyers)

Teams inside larger orgs, and small companies.

Jobs-to-be-done:

- Subscribe to curated registries that improve retrieval and decisions
- Keep knowledge current without adding meetings
- Bring external expertise into their node, with clear boundaries

Buying triggers:

- High uncertainty, high cost of mistakes
- Domain shifts faster than their internal docs can keep up
- They already pay for newsletters/courses but can’t integrate it into workflows

## Tertiary audience: Individuals (buyers)

Power users:

- researchers, founders, product folks, engineers
- people who already maintain personal knowledge workflows

Jobs-to-be-done:

- Plug in high-signal context feeds without changing tools
- Pull "just enough" context when needed

## Enterprise stakeholders (supporting audience)

Not the initial buyer, but a frequent constraint.

Care-abouts:

- SSO/SCIM, audit logs
- policy controls and rate limits
- vendor risk and compliance posture
- invoicing and predictable billing
